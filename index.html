Metrics library

The metrics that expose operational gaps.

Productivity, accuracy, speed, cost, workforce, and forecasting metrics with standard formulas, plus what each one reveals when it moves in the wrong direction.

31 metrics

Productivity

Parcels per labor hour

Parcels processed ÷ Total labor hours

Reveals: shifts or teams with low throughput, and staffing that does not match workload.

Productivity

Output per head per shift

Parcels processed ÷ (Headcount × Shifts)

Reveals: performance spread between people and teams, and where coaching is needed.

Productivity

Capacity utilization

(Actual volume ÷ Rated capacity) × 100

Reveals: idle capacity, overload, and bottleneck stations in the flow.

Productivity

Overtime ratio

(Overtime hours ÷ Regular hours) × 100

Reveals: chronic under-staffing, weak rostering, and hidden labor cost.

Productivity

Backlog per labor hour

Pending parcels ÷ Available labor hours

Reveals: how many hours of work are queued up and whether the team can clear it.

Quality & Accuracy

Sorting accuracy

(Correctly sorted ÷ Total sorted) × 100

Reveals: how reliable sorting is by shift, lane, or operator.

Quality & Accuracy

Missort / error rate

(Missorted parcels ÷ Total parcels) × 100

Reveals: unclear destination logic, SOP gaps, training gaps, or layout problems.

Quality & Accuracy

First-pass data accuracy

(Records with no correction ÷ Total records) × 100

Reveals: data-entry quality and where validation rules are missing.

Quality & Accuracy

Inventory accuracy

(Matched parcels ÷ Audited parcels) × 100

Reveals: gaps between physical stock and system records, and weak scan discipline.

Quality & Accuracy

Rework rate

(Reprocessed parcels ÷ Total parcels) × 100

Reveals: how much capacity is lost to repeating work, and which causes repeat most.

Quality & Accuracy

DPMO (Six Sigma)

(Defects ÷ (Units × Opportunities)) × 1,000,000

Reveals: true process capability, comparable across teams of different size.

Speed & SLA

SLA compliance

(On-time parcels ÷ Total parcels) × 100

Reveals: which stage of the flow is causing late parcels.

Speed & SLA

Turnaround time (TAT)

Average (Completion time − Start time)

Reveals: slow handovers, queueing, and stage-wise delays.

Speed & SLA

Backlog aging

Pending parcels grouped by age: >24h, >48h, >72h

Reveals: stuck parcels and exceptions with no clear owner.

Speed & SLA

Parcel dwell time

Average hours a parcel stays in the hub

Reveals: slow dispatch, poor load planning, and storage congestion.

Speed & SLA

Case resolution time

Average (Case closed − Case opened)

Reveals: investigation and escalation bottlenecks, and repeat root causes.

Cost Analysis

Cost per parcel

Total operating cost ÷ Parcels processed

Reveals: overall efficiency, and the effect of volume swings on unit cost.

Cost Analysis

Labor cost per parcel

Total labor cost ÷ Parcels processed

Reveals: whether manpower is over- or under-sized for the volume handled.

Cost Analysis

Cost of poor quality (COPQ)

Rework cost + Loss/damage + Claims + Re-delivery cost

Reveals: the true price of errors, which helps prioritize quality projects.

Cost Analysis

Overtime cost share

(Overtime cost ÷ Total labor cost) × 100

Reveals: how much spend comes from avoidable overtime.

Cost Analysis

Budget variance

Actual cost − Budgeted cost

Reveals: cost lines drifting from plan, so action can start early.

Workforce & Turnover

Employee turnover rate

(Separations ÷ Average headcount) × 100

Reveals: retention problems by team, shift, or supervisor, and the cost of replacing people.

Workforce & Turnover

Early attrition (first 90 days)

(Leavers within 90 days ÷ New hires) × 100

Reveals: weak hiring fit or onboarding gaps.

Workforce & Turnover

Absenteeism rate

(Absent days ÷ Scheduled work days) × 100

Reveals: engagement issues, shift fatigue, and unplanned capacity loss.

Workforce & Turnover

Span of control

Staff count ÷ Number of supervisors

Reveals: supervision overload or under-use, which affects coaching and quality.

Workforce & Turnover

Time to proficiency

Average days from joining to target productivity

Reveals: training effectiveness and the real cost of high turnover.

Forecasting

Forecast accuracy

100 − MAPE, where MAPE = Avg(|Actual − Forecast| ÷ Actual) × 100

Reveals: how reliable volume planning is, and which days or clients cause misses.

Forecasting

Forecast bias

((Forecast − Actual) ÷ Actual) × 100

Reveals: whether planning consistently over- or under-estimates volume.

Forecasting

Required headcount

Forecast parcels ÷ (Parcels per labor hour × Productive hours per shift) × (1 + Absence buffer)

Reveals: manpower needed per shift before volume arrives.

Forecasting

Peak-load index

Peak-day volume ÷ Average-day volume

Reveals: how much surge capacity or flexible manpower the operation needs.

Forecasting

Capacity gap

Forecast volume − Rated capacity

Reveals: expected shortfall or surplus, so decisions can be made ahead of time.

Reading metrics together to find the gap

A single number rarely explains the problem. These combinations point to the most likely gap and a sensible first action.

Signal patternLikely gapFirst action
Low parcels per labor hour + high overtime ratioRostering mismatch or a bottleneck at one stationTime-and-motion check; rebalance shifts and stations
High missort rate + low training completionSkill or SOP knowledge gapTargeted retraining; checklist at the sorting point
High turnover + rising reworkLoss of experience and weak onboardingStructured onboarding, buddy system, exit-reason analysis
High cost per parcel + low capacity utilizationExcess fixed capacity or idle hoursRebalance volume; adjust headcount and shift pattern
Large forecast error + backlog spikesPlanning gapImprove forecasting method; keep a flexible manpower pool
SLA dip + growing backlog agingHandover or escalation failureClear ownership, aging alerts, daily review meeting
Low inventory accuracy + many unlocated parcelsScan discipline gapScan-compliance audits and regular cycle counts

Formulas are standard operational definitions. Targets and thresholds should be set from your own verified baseline data.
