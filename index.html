Lean & Six Sigma

Find the Bottleneck. Fix the Process. Improve the Outcome.

  1. Identify operational problems
  2. Analyze data and root causes
  3. Remove unnecessary process steps
  4. Standardize work using SOPs
  5. Train the relevant team
  6. Track performance through KPIs
  7. Review results and sustain improvements

Before (illustrative)

  • Manual handoffs and unclear ownership
  • Errors found late, rework repeats

After (illustrative)
